Projects

My PhD is on how an mRNA’s sequence sets how much protein a cell makes from it. The first three projects make up that work.

seq2te Active

Predicts a gene’s translation efficiency from its mRNA sequence in a given cell type, and shows which parts of the sequence carry the signal. It combines ribosome footprint coverage, gene and isoform translation efficiency, RNA structure, RNA-binding protein motifs and protein features, and tests transfer to unseen cell types. This is the methods and benchmark paper of my PhD, and it grew out of proTE.

genTE Active

Designs the mRNA for a given protein so that it translates with a chosen profile in a chosen cell state. The protein stays fixed and only the message changes. Each design is scored by a model independent of the one that proposed it.

TE labels Active

Translation efficiency labels from public matched Ribo-seq and RNA-seq studies, per gene, per isoform and between conditions, each level with its own quality checks. They train and evaluate seq2te and genTE.

riboseq-agent Contributor

Owen Rackham’s project to find and process public ribosome profiling studies. I contribute the Nextflow processing and the coverage tracks.

kitsune Group project

A UoSDDNB group project that fine-tunes nucleotide sequence models to find unannotated small open reading frames.

locus Group project

UoSDDNB group work. locus finds public omics datasets from a plain-English question and reads each one’s sample records to check whether it fits.
