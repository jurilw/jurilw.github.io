Juri Westendorf

I am a computational biology PhD researcher at the University of Southampton, in Professor Owen J. Rackham’s group.

My PhD asks how much of a protein’s output is written in the sequence of its mRNA. I build models that predict translation efficiency from sequence in a given cell type, and I am starting to use them to design mRNAs with a chosen translation profile.
