Juri Westendorf

I am a computational biology PhD researcher at the University of Southampton, in Professor Owen J. Rackham’s group.

My research focuses on the genetic-sequence features that determine protein output. I develop proTE, which integrates matched RNA-seq and Ribo-seq data with biological foundation-model representations and interpretable sequence features.
