Research

  • Computational biology PhD researcher at the University of Southampton, in Professor Owen J. Rackham’s group.
  • Research on how the sequence of an mRNA sets how much protein is made from it.
  • Developer of seq2te, which predicts translation efficiency from mRNA sequence across cell types, and genTE, which designs mRNAs with a chosen translation profile.
  • Contributor to riboseq-agent (Owen Rackham’s group) and to the UoSDDNB group projects kitsune and locus.
