Plans

Two papers come out of my PhD. The first, seq2te, is a methods and benchmark paper on predicting translation efficiency from mRNA sequence across cell types. It fixes the labels, data splits and evaluation that the second paper reuses.

The second, genTE, turns prediction into design: choosing the mRNA for a given protein so that it translates as intended in a given cell state. The goal is designs that hold up when they are measured in cells.
